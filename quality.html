Quality Control

Miller Tool and Die practices "Total Quality Management." Our quality processes have been audited, and approved, by leading aerospace companies.

Quality control is involved in all phases of our operation. From initial design to final packaging and shipping.

Our quality system is compliant with ISO 9001:2000/AS9100. Our personnel are trained to perform applicable phases of quality functions, such as:

  • Dead Load Testing
  • Dye Penetrant Inspection
  • Rockwell Testing
  • Interpretation of Geometric Feature Controls
  • Inspection and Calibration

Our comprehensive quality manual can be made available to your company upon request.
