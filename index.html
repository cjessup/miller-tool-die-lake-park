Miller Tool & Die is a full service Design and Fabrication facility located in Lake Park, Florida. Our expertise in tools, dies, jigs, fixtures, gages, prototypes and specialty machining ranges from intricate parts and fixturing for the medical field to large support tooling and ground handling equipment for the aerospace industry. Experienced designers are proficient running various CAD packages including "Unigraphics" and "Autocad". We also have mechanical engineers available to run "Pro Mechanica" for your analysis needs. Machine shop capabilities include CNC milling, EDM, turning, heat treat, welding and comprehensive quality control. It would be our pleasure to provide you with a quotation for your next project.
